# Calm Chinese-inspired brand voice

Use this voice only when requested or when the supplied brand material clearly calls for it. Otherwise derive the voice from the current brief instead of imposing this aesthetic.

## Voice coordinates

- Calm rather than passive.
- Refined rather than ornate.
- Warm rather than intimate without permission.
- Sensory and concrete rather than mystical and vague.
- Spacious rather than fragmentary.
- Invitational rather than promotional or urgent.

The default visual world may draw from natural light, stone, wood, tea steam, water, breath, sound, seasonal change, and small daily rituals. Use one coherent image at a time.

## Chinese rhythm

- Mix short sentences with one slightly longer explanatory sentence.
- Let an image open the thought, then land it in a real object, action, or scene.
- Use white space for pacing; do not turn every phrase into a separate line.
- Prefer restrained verbs: 放下、靠近、听见、照见、安放、留一刻、慢下来.
- Avoid empty stacks such as 疗愈能量、宇宙频率、灵魂觉醒 unless the brand explicitly uses them and the context is clearly symbolic.

Useful movement:

`scene → object/action → felt meaning → quiet invitation`

## English rhythm

Prefer grounded lines such as:

- “A small ritual for slower evenings.”
- “Made to mark a pause, not promise a cure.”
- “Stone, sound, and a moment to return to yourself.”

Avoid pseudo-profound literal translations and repeated “healing” claims. Suitable words include stillness, grounding, quiet, ritual, pause, texture, resonance, presence, and intention when the surrounding sentence makes them concrete.

## Domain claim boundaries

### Crystals and bracelets

Describe color, texture, cultural symbolism, design intention, wearing ritual, or the user's reported feeling. Do not claim that a stone treats anxiety, changes hormones, guarantees luck, attracts money, or produces a medical/financial result.

Safer framing:

- “a reminder to…”
- “traditionally associated with…” when that tradition is actually sourced;
- “designed around the intention of…”
- “some people use it as part of…”

### Tea

Describe origin, cultivar, processing, aroma, taste, brewing, and scene only when supplied. Avoid detox, weight-loss, disease-prevention, sleep, or other health promises without appropriate evidence and authorization.

### Meditation and sound

Describe practice length, instruments, sound texture, setting, and personal experience. Do not present meditation or sound as a substitute for medical or mental-health care, or guarantee sleep, anxiety relief, or trauma recovery.

## Source priority

This reference supplies style, never product facts. User-provided brand guidelines override its wording. If the user's approved vocabulary conflicts with a safety boundary, preserve the intent while removing or qualifying the unsupported claim.
